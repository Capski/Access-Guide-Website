4. **Navigation:** Is the Navbar sticky/fixed at the top? (Rubric: Navigasi intuitif).
5. **Typography:** Are the fonts consistent with the design system? (Rubric: Typography konsisten).
6. **Color Scheme:** Are the colors consistent with the design system? (Rubric: Warna konsisten).
7. **Layout:** Is the layout consistent with the design system? (Rubric: Layout konsisten).
